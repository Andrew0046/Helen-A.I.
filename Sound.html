Letter ladder

A = 80 Hz; each next letter is 8.8 Hz higher; Z = 300 Hz. The composite number uses four positions in base 27: empty = 0, A = 1 through Z = 26. It uniquely identifies the arrangement; you can assign its meaning above. Each letter owns the range halfway to its neighbors (±4.4 Hz), with the ends limited to 80–300 Hz for this test.
