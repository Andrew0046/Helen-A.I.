How to use it

Mic listens for up to two notes at a time. Play cleanly, one or two keys, and raise sensitivity if the room is noisy.

Tap piano records the on-screen keys (or computer keys). Hold a key to hold the note.

Import audio analyzes an MP3/WAV for up to two simultaneous piano voices, shows the waveform, and lets you compare Original / Detected / Both.

Practice loads written note names and listens to your real piano through the microphone. Correct notes advance automatically; repeated notes require a release before the next step.

Computer keys: A S D F G H J K white keys from C4. Black keys: W E T Y U. Space starts/stops recording. Z undo.

Piano roll: select and drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to resize it, or use the editor buttons. Quantize snaps timing to the BPM grid.

Export MIDI into a DAW, or CSV/JSON for study. MIDI now follows the selected BPM. Transpose changes playback and MIDI, not the captured list.
